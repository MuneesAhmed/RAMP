Getting Started

Welcome to the Railway Accommodation Maintenance Portal (RAMP). This guide will help you understand how to use the portal effectively for submitting and tracking maintenance requests for railway accommodations.

Key Features:
  • Submit maintenance requests for railway accommodations
  • Track the status of your requests
  • Access the staff portal for administrative functions
  • View maintenance history and updates

Submitting a Request

  1. Go to the homepage and click on "Submit Request"
  2. Select your division and city from the dropdown menus
  3. Choose your colony and quarters number
  4. Select the department relevant to your maintenance issue
  5. Describe the problem in detail
  6. Upload photos of the issue (optional)
  7. Submit your request
Keep your request ID safe for future tracking.

Tracking Your Request

To track your maintenance request:

  1. Click on "Track Your Request" in the navigation menu
  2. Enter your request ID and registered mobile number
  3. View the current status and history of your request
Request Status Meanings:
  • Pending - Request received but not yet assigned
  • Not Operable - Issue cannot be resolved
  • Resolved - Maintenance work completed
  • Forwarded to Other Department - Request transferred to another department

Staff Portal

The staff portal is accessible only to authorized personnel:

  • Administrators: Can view statistics, manage supervisors, and oversee all requests
  • Supervisors: Can view and update assigned maintenance requests
Access to the staff portal requires valid credentials.

Frequently Asked Questions

Request processing time varies depending on the type of maintenance required. Most requests are assigned to a supervisor within 24 hours.

Contact support with your registered mobile number and quarters details. They can help retrieve your request ID.

Once submitted, requests cannot be edited. However, you can add comments or additional information by contacting support.
